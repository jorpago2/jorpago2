Research

Integrated photonics, advanced materials and emerging devices

My research explores how functional materials and novel device concepts can enable reconfigurable and energy-efficient photonic and electronic systems, with applications in sensing, information processing and neuromorphic computing.

Research areas

01

Integrated photonics with advanced materials

Reconfigurable and non-volatile photonic devices that combine silicon platforms with functional materials such as phase-change compounds and metal oxides.

02

Neuromorphic photonic and electronic hardware

Devices and architectures for analogue, in-memory and brain-inspired information processing, including optical activation functions and spiking devices.

03

Emerging optoelectronic devices and characterization

Design, modelling and experimental characterization of photonic and semiconductor devices, together with their electronic control and measurement systems.

Approach: device modelling, electromagnetic simulation, circuit design and experimental characterization.

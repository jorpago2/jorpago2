Research overview

Research

Reconfigurable integrated photonics with functional materials

My research focuses on silicon photonic devices enhanced with functional materials. I explore how light can be switched, controlled and stored efficiently, and how these devices can support neuromorphic information processing.

Research areas

01

Functional materials for integrated photonics

Hybrid silicon and silicon nitride platforms incorporating vanadium dioxide, transparent conducting oxides and phase-change materials to introduce active optical functionality.

02

Reconfigurable and non-volatile photonic devices

Integrated switches, phase shifters, photonic memories and polarization-control devices designed for compact, energy-efficient and robust operation.

03

Neuromorphic photonic hardware

Integrated nonlinear activation functions, spiking devices and multilevel memories for analogue and brain-inspired optical information processing.

Approach: device modelling, electromagnetic simulation, numerical optimization and experimental characterization.

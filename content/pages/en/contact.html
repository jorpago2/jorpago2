Student projects

BSc and MSc students

If you are a bachelor’s or master’s student looking for a thesis project (TFG/TFM), feel free to get in touch. I am happy to discuss potential topics and ideas with you.

Potential project topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Automated measurement and characterisation Measurement systems for advanced semiconductors, photodetectors, phototransistors and emerging optoelectronic devices.
  • Electronic interfaces for optical devices Signal-conditioning circuits for optical and optoelectronic devices.
  • Integrated photonic circuit control Electronic circuits for the control and reconfiguration of integrated photonic systems.
  • Memristive and emerging devices Memristors and electronic devices with novel functionalities.
  • Neuromorphic hardware Electronic and photonic devices for neuromorphic computing based on advanced semiconductors.

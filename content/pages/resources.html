Links and references

Learning and data

04
  • Branch Education. YouTube channel with insightful 3D animations explaining science, engineering and modern technology.
  • Virtual lab of integrated photonic building blocks. Interactive web simulations for building intuition about photonic integrated circuit components.
  • Epoch AI. Updated data and resources about the growth and impact of artificial intelligence.
  • 2D Trends. Semiconductor transistor trends from the Pop Lab at Stanford.

Design, simulation and measurement

05
  • Tidy3D. Fast cloud-based electromagnetic simulation with web and Python interfaces and extensive photonics examples.
  • Refractiveindex.info. Comprehensive database of optical constants.
  • GDSFactory. Python library for designing photonic, electronic, quantum and MEMS chips and generating CAD files.
  • PreFab. Machine-learning tools for predicting and correcting nanoscale photonic fabrication outcomes.
  • PyVISA. Python package for controlling measurement instruments over GPIB, RS232, USB or Ethernet.

Further reading and collections

05

  • Attention residue and task switching. Research on why switching between tasks makes focused work difficult.

Fabrication

08
  • KLayout. Free layout viewer and editor compatible with GDS and OASIS files.
  • NanoWiki. Equipment manuals, recipes and safety procedures from Leiden University’s NanoLab group.
  • Nanolithography. Detailed fabrication processes and recipes from Georgia Tech’s Institute for Electronics and Nanotechnology.
  • Nanofab-net. Open platform for sharing nanofabrication processes and cleanroom information.
  • Sam Zeloof. Homemade semiconductor fabrication and related projects. See also his website.
  • Projects in Flight. Semiconductor projects and practical insights into homemade IC fabrication. See also the blog.
  • HackerFab. Open guides, fabrication processes and fab toolkits inspired by Sam Zeloof’s work.
  • FabuBlox. Design and data-management tools for micro- and nanofabrication processes.

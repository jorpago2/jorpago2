# Blog editorial notes

- Audience: undergraduate, master's and PhD students, including readers outside the article's specialty.
- Write in clear, natural language. Explain a technical term when first needed, using short, precise examples rather than promotional language.
- Take the approach of a journal News & Views piece. Give enough context to understand the contribution, and develop the comment around a specific result, design choice or open question. Distinguish experiments, simulations and estimates.
- Write connected paragraphs that relate the evidence to the interpretation. Let the commentary stand on its own and describe the figures in their captions.
- Base substantial reviews on accessible full texts. Papers without an accessible full text may be mentioned briefly, with the basis of the mention made clear.
- Keep the English, Spanish and Valencian versions consistent. Attribute article figures and verify their reuse licences.
- Name and link the authors' participating institutions with their countries, using the affiliations stated in the paper.
- Explain basic concepts in the text. Reserve supplementary links for novel methods, results and directly relevant coverage. Identify the source and label links in another language.
- Use discourse connectors to make the logical relationship between sentences and paragraphs clear. Choose them for their meaning and keep them natural.
- Do not use colons in blog prose, headlines or figure captions. Restructure sentences to keep the prose natural.

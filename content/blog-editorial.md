# Blog editorial notes

- Audience: undergraduate, master's and PhD students, including readers outside the article's specialty.
- Write in clear, natural language. Explain a technical term when first needed, using short, precise examples rather than promotional language.
- Explain the question, how the work addresses it, what was actually demonstrated, and an important limitation where relevant. Distinguish experiments, simulations and estimates.
- Base substantial reviews on accessible full texts. Papers without an accessible full text may be mentioned briefly, with the basis of the mention made clear.
- Keep the English, Spanish and Valencian versions consistent. Attribute article figures and verify their reuse licences.
- When useful, link explained concepts to reliable teaching materials and provide relevant, clearly identified news or institutional coverage. Prefer accessible sources in the reader's language and label links in another language.
